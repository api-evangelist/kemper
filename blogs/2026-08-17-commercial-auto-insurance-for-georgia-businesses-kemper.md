Running a small business in Georgia means your truck, van or work vehicle is on the road every single day, and that means your risk is on the road every single day too. Commercial auto insurance policies in Georgia are made to cover the vehicles that keep your business moving, from the moment you load your first ladder or lawnmower in the morning to the last delivery of the night. Kemper Auto Commercial answers the questions every business owner asks before buying a policy: What does Georgia require, what does a policy actually cover, how much will it cost, and how fast can I get insured?
