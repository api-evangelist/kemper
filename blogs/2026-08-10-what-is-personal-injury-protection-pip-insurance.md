Insurance terms can be unfamiliar, and now you have come across a new one: Personal Injury Protection (PIP) insurance. What is PIP insurance? Depending on where you live, PIP can be either optional or required auto insurance coverage that helps pay medical expenses and other eligible costs after a covered car accident, often regardless of who caused the crash.
